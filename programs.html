Kids Swimming Program

Zero Water Fear · Safe Buoyancy · 25m Unassisted Freestyle & Backstroke

Our Kids Program transforms non-swimmers and timid children into joyful, safe, and technically competent swimmers. Conducted in our 32°C temperature-regulated learner pool with shallow ledge entries, our certified child instructors use play-based biomechanics, progressive floating aids, and reassuring 1-on-1 attention so every child gains effortless propulsion without fear.

Water Confidence

Rhythmic bubble blowing, stress-free head submersion & relaxed floating.

Freestyle & Backstroke

Continuous flutter kick, side bilateral breathing & streamlined arm pull.

Essential Water Safety

Treading water, roll-to-breathe recovery, pool edge climbs & safety jumps.

Monthly Milestone Badges

Regular skill assessment reports and graduation certificates for every level.

Teens Swimming Program

4-Stroke Mastery · Tumble Flip Turns · Cardiovascular Fitness & Aerobic Power

Tailored for teenagers seeking fitness, school swim team preparation, or stroke perfection. This curriculum emphasizes hydro-dynamic body positioning, high-elbow catch efficiency, underwater dolphin kick propulsion, and tumble turns. Swimmers build serious cardiovascular capacity while mastering all four competitive strokes with zero shoulder strain.

Full 4-Stroke Technique

Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke whip & Butterfly undulation mechanics.

Turns & Block Dives

Somersault flip turns, backstroke push-offs & streamlined dive entries.

Aerobic Stamina & Speed

Paced interval sets, heart-rate zone swimming & lactic recovery drills.

Video Stroke Analysis

High-speed underwater camera reviews to eliminate drag and boost speed.

Adult Swimming Program

Fear-Free Water Acclimation · Low-Impact Joint Conditioning · Triathlon Lap Training

It is never too late to experience the effortless freedom of swimming. Whether overcoming lifelong aquaphobia, recovering from joint surgery with zero-gravity aquatic exercise, or training for a corporate sprint triathlon, our patient adult specialists tailor every drill to your comfort level in a dignified, adult-only atmosphere.

Aquaphobia Relief

Gentle breath control, standing-depth drills & relaxed balance floating.

Effortless Oxygen Intake

Bow-wave side breathing technique without neck strain or lifting.

Low-Impact Cardio

Burn 500+ kcal/hr with 90% reduced stress on knees, hips, and back.

Masters & Triathlon Pacing

Open water sighting, drafting tactics & CSS threshold interval pace sets.
