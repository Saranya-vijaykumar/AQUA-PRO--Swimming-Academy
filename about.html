Founded in 2008 · Southern California

More Than Swimming Lessons.
A Lifelong Superpower.

What began as an intimate community learn-to-swim program has evolved into a premier aquatic academy trusted by over 15,000 families. From a toddler taking their first fearless submersions to state championship finalists breaking record times, Aquarium Pro is built on the philosophy that every human deserves confidence in deep water.

We operate with strict coach-to-student ratios (1:4 for toddlers, 1:6 for youth), FINA-compliant biomechanical video feedback, and therapeutic 32°C heated teaching water. That uncompromising commitment to safety and quality is why 95% of our families continue season after season.
